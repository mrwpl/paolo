About the conference

This two-day conference celebrates the eightieth birthday of Fedor Bogomolov, one of the most original and influential geometers of our time. Bogomolov's work has shaped algebraic geometry for five decades, from the foundational study of holomorphic symplectic manifolds and Bogomolov–Beauville decomposition, to his profound contributions to birational geometry, the geometry of algebraic curves, and Galois theory.

Bringing together leading mathematicians whose work intersects with and has been inspired by Bogomolov's, the conference will feature research talks across the themes that have defined his career. All are welcome to attend.
