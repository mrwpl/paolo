Read from ingredients to conclusions.

Every arrow points from a supporting result to a theorem it helps prove. The classification is the ultimate destination. Dashed arrows indicate alternative strategies, which are not all required at once.

Choose a book panel for a manageable part of the proof, or open the full atlas. Click a result to see its hypotheses, conclusion, Lean declarations, provenance and saved evidence. The highlighted prerequisite chain traces what a result needs; its consequences trace what it can support.

Verified means the stated scope has recorded verification evidence. Compiled is weaker: the module built but its complete audit was still pending at the snapshot. Open means not yet verified in this project, not that the result is absent from every Lean library.

These are mathematical milestones, not equal units of effort. Node counts are not a completion percentage. Statements about models, arbitrary members and deformation families are kept separate.

The atlas is a dated snapshot. It does not monitor a running agent or perform new Lean checks.
